## 🚀 Currently Building

### Exoper

**Exoper** is an AI-powered trading technology platform designed to help traders analyze markets, build structured trading systems, manage execution, and improve their trading process.

🌐 **Website:** https://exoper.com

The platform combines:

- Technical market analysis
- Macroeconomic analysis
- Trading systems and rulebooks
- Automated and manual execution
- Trade management
- Risk management
- Portfolio analysis
- Trading journals
- Performance analytics
- Behavioral and discipline analysis
- AI-assisted decision support

The goal is to build a structured technology layer around the trading workflow rather than simply provide another indicator or trading bot.
